01 — About

From lab bench to dashboard

My background is in physics — trained to measure carefully, question assumptions, and let the data settle the argument. That habit of mind carried straight through three moves: data analyst, then data scientist, and now applied AI.

Each stage built on the last. As an analyst I learned to clean, model, and report on data in SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI. As a data scientist I moved from describing what happened to modelling what's likely to happen next. Now I'm focused on applied AI — building on that same analytical foundation to ship AI-powered tools rather than just insight.
